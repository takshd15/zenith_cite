Questions

GEO without the shortcuts.

Is GEO different from SEO?
The work overlaps substantially. Technical accessibility and useful, reliable content support traditional search and AI search features. Zenith adds documented testing of AI answers and named competitor visibility.
Do we need special AI schema?
No special “AI schema” is required. Structured data should accurately describe visible content and follow the requirements for the relevant schema type.
How do you measure progress?
We repeat the agreed question set, record answer outcomes and sources, document completed implementation, and use website analytics where tracking allows.
Can you guarantee an AI recommendation?
No. AI platforms control their answers, which can vary. Zenith commits to the agreed work and transparent reporting.
