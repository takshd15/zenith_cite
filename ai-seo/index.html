AI search

AI SEO for the answers your buyers read.

AI SEO is the work of making your business easy for AI search tools to find, understand and cite. When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews for a recommendation, the answer is assembled from web pages, listings and reviews. It improves the parts of that picture you can influence.

Zenith combines proven search foundations with documented testing of the answers themselves, so you can see what changed and why.

Same foundations, new checks.

Most AI search optimization builds on good SEO. What changes is how the results are tested, measured and reported.

Old and new

what changes, and what stays the same.

Google advises that the same foundational SEO best practices apply to its AI features. Crawlable pages, helpful content and accurate business information still matter most. AI answers add a new layer: they combine several sources into one response, so what other websites say about you carries more weight.

Stays the same

technical foundations

Pages that load, render and can be crawled and indexed, with clear titles, headings, internal links and accurate structured data.

Matters more

consistent public information

Your services, prices, locations and expertise should match across your website, business profiles, directories and reviews.

New to measure

answers, not just rankings

We track brand mentions, recommendations and cited links separately, because an AI answer has no single ranking position.

AI search optimization in practice

from tested questions to website changes.

AI search optimization follows a repeatable loop. Every recommendation is tied to an observed answer, a cited source or a technical requirement.

01

build the question set

Agree 15–20 questions your customers actually ask, such as “best accountant for startups in Amsterdam” or “alternatives to [competitor]”.

02

review answers and sources

Record which businesses appear, how they are described and which pages and third-party sites the answers cite.

03

fix the gaps

Improve service pages, comparison content, business information and technical issues that keep you out of the answer.

04

repeat and report

Re-test the same questions, document what changed and set the next priorities. Normal answer variation is not reported as impact.

Without shortcuts

optimizing AI search results the right way.

Optimizing AI search results means improving the information those systems rely on, not trying to trick the output. Some tactics promise quick wins and create lasting risk.

We do

useful, accurate content

Pages that answer real buying questions with specific, verifiable facts that a customer can act on.

We do not

hidden text or prompt tricks

No hidden instructions for AI crawlers, keyword stuffing, fake reviews or invented statistics.

We do not

promise placements

AI platforms control their answers. We commit to agreed work and transparent reporting instead.

Questions

common questions.

Does AI SEO replace traditional SEO?
No. Strong traditional SEO is the foundation. It adds testing of AI-generated answers, a closer look at the sources they cite and work on the third-party information those answers draw on.
Which AI platforms do you test?
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. We record the date, platform and context of every test because answers change over time.
How long before results appear?
The audit gives you a baseline within 48–72 hours after we receive the required information. Changes in AI answers usually take weeks to months and depend on your website, competition and the platforms.
