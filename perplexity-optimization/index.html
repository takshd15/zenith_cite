Perplexity optimization

Perplexity optimization for cited answers.

Perplexity searches the web to answer questions and shows its sources as numbered citations next to the answer. Perplexity optimization is about making your pages reachable, specific and useful enough to be one of those sources.

Because every citation links to a page, being cited in Perplexity can send interested visitors straight to your website.

Citations you can see.

Perplexity shows its sources, which makes it one of the easiest AI platforms to measure accurately.

How Perplexity works

why sources matter more here.

Perplexity bases its answers on current web results and shows where each part of the answer came from. That makes source quality and access especially important.

Live search

answers from current pages

Perplexity looks up sources when you ask, so recent updates to your website can appear sooner than in training data.

Visible citations

every claim has a source

Numbered citations link to the pages used. Being one of them is a measurable outcome with a clear link to your site.

PerplexityBot

the crawler that indexes you

Perplexity uses PerplexityBot to discover and index pages. If robots.txt blocks it, your pages are unlikely to be cited.

Questions

Perplexity questions.

Is Perplexity optimization different from SEO?
It overlaps a lot. Perplexity relies on web search, so crawlable, specific and well-structured pages help in both. The difference is that we measure citations in Perplexity answers rather than rankings alone.
How do I know whether Perplexity cites my website?
Ask the questions your customers ask and look at the numbered sources. Our audit does this systematically, with dated records for each question.
Can you guarantee Perplexity citations?
No. Perplexity decides which sources to use. We commit to agreed improvements and transparent reporting of what changes.
