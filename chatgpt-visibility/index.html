How ChatGPT answers

where ChatGPT gets its information.

ChatGPT can answer from what it learned during training or by searching the web. Both routes affect whether your business is mentioned.

Training data

what the model already knows

ChatGPT search

Improving ChatGPT findability

a clear, repeatable process.

ChatGPT findability improves when your website is accessible, specific and supported by reliable independent sources.

01

check access

Confirm that OAI-SearchBot can crawl your important pages and that nothing in robots.txt or your hosting setup blocks it.

02

No. OpenAI controls ChatGPT’s answers, and they vary between users and conversations. We commit to an agreed scope of work and transparent reporting.

Answers depend on the wording, conversation context, whether web search is used and model updates. That is why we test a fixed set of questions repeatedly and record the date of each test.
