What shapes the answer

what AI tools look at for law firms.

Legal answers tend to cite authoritative sources. Your own website is one of them, but rarely the only one.

01

practice-area pages

A page per practice area, explaining which cases you take, for whom and how the process works, is easier to match to a question.

02

lawyer profiles

Profiles with qualifications, bar admission, languages and focus areas help AI tools connect expertise to named people.

03

legal directories and rankings

Independent directories, rankings and bar association listings often appear as sources for recommendation questions.

04

published guidance

Articles and guides that answer common legal questions clearly can be cited directly in AI answers.

Questions

common questions.

Will AI tools recommend a specific law firm?
Often yes. For many legal questions, AI answers name firms or point to directories. Our audit records which firms appear for your practice areas and why.
Do directory rankings matter for AI answers?
They can. Independent directories and rankings are frequently used as sources. We check how your firm is listed and whether the details are consistent.
Can you work within our compliance rules?
Yes. We work from your firm’s rules and your bar’s advertising guidance, and every page is approved by your lawyers before publication.
