What shapes the answer

what AI tools look at for clinics.

AI answers about local healthcare combine several sources. Gaps or contradictions in any of them can keep a good clinic out of the answer.

01

Google Business Profile and reviews

Opening hours, services, photos and review volume and recency are visible signals for local answers.

02

treatment pages

A dedicated page for each treatment, explaining who it suits, what happens and what it costs, is easier to cite than a single services list.

03

consistent practice details

Your name, address, phone number, hours and payment or insurance information should match everywhere they appear.

04

health directories and review platforms

National directories and patient review sites often appear as sources and should describe your clinic accurately.

What we do

practical work for dental practices.

We focus on accurate, useful information rather than marketing claims about treatment results.

01

treatment page structure

One clear page per key treatment, with process, suitability, aftercare and price ranges where you publish them.

02

local information audit

Check and align your practice details across your website, Google Business Profile and directories.

03

multi-location setup

For groups, a page per location with its own team, hours and services, so AI can match the right clinic to the right area.

Rules on healthcare advertising differ between EU countries. Your dentists review and approve any clinical content before it is published, and we do not write claims about guaranteed outcomes.
