FAQs

AI visibility FAQ

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility describes whether and how your business appears in AI-generated answers. That can include a brand mention, a recommendation, or a link to your website.

These are different outcomes, and we report them separately.

What is generative engine optimisation?

Generative engine optimisation, often called GEO, is the practice of improving how a business can be discovered and represented in AI-generated answers.

Our work focuses on useful content, clear business information, technical accessibility, and documented testing.

Is this different from SEO?

The work overlaps substantially. A technically accessible website and useful, reliable content support traditional search and Google’s AI search features.

Our service adds testing of AI-generated answers and competitor visibility to help identify further priorities.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. AI platforms control their answers, and those answers can vary between searches and users.

We commit to an agreed scope of work and transparent reporting.

How quickly will I see results?

The audit provides an initial picture within 48–72 hours after the required information is received.

Changes in search visibility take longer and depend on your website, competition, implementation, and the platforms themselves. We establish a baseline and track progress over time.

Do I need a new website?

Usually, we begin by assessing your existing website. We identify which changes can be made within your current setup before recommending a rebuild.

What happens during the free snapshot?

We explore three relevant customer questions in ChatGPT and Google, discuss what appears, and explain whether a full audit would be useful.

It is a short initial review, separate from the paid audit.

Is there a minimum contract?

Growth and Authority start with a three-month minimum term, which gives technical and content changes time to take effect.

After that, plans continue month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days’ notice. The audit is a one-time purchase with no ongoing commitment.

How will we track progress?

Each month you receive a written report showing the work completed, the re-tested customer questions, and any changes in mentions, recommendations and cited links.

Where your analytics allow it, we also report visits and enquiries from AI platforms and search.

How do I get a quote?

Use the booking form and choose “Custom quote” as your interest. Tell us about your websites, markets and goals, and we reply within one business day with next steps or a written proposal.

Which markets do you cover?

We work remotely with businesses across the European Union. We test customer questions in English and Dutch, and compare answers between countries where that matters.

Programmes covering several countries are scoped as a Custom plan. See working across the EU.
