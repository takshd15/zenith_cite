an ai visibility agency for the answers your customers read

Zenith Cite works on one problem: your AI visibility. People now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews which company to pick, and the three or four names in that answer become the shortlist. If you are not one of them, most of those buyers never reach your website.

The work is AI search engine optimization, which you will also see called generative engine optimisation (GEO). In practice we ask the AI tools the same questions your buyers ask, note who gets named and which pages get cited, and then fix the gaps on your site that explain why it is not you.

If you are looking for a geo bureau, that is the service. You get dated screenshots, a short list of what to fix first, and reporting that shows what moved. We do not promise rankings, because nobody can.

FAQs

AI visibility FAQ

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is whether your business shows up when someone asks an AI tool for help, and how it is described when it does. Showing up can mean a passing mention, a recommendation, or a link to your site.

Those are different results, so we count them separately.

What is generative engine optimisation?

Generative engine optimisation, or GEO, is work that makes it easier for AI tools to find your business and describe it correctly.

For us that means useful pages, consistent business details, a site that crawlers can read, and testing that is written down.

Is this different from SEO?

Mostly it is the same work. A site that loads, can be crawled and answers real questions does well in normal search and in Google’s AI features.

What we add is testing the AI answers themselves and comparing you with named competitors, which shows gaps a normal SEO report misses.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. The AI companies decide what their tools say, and the answer to the same question changes between people and days.

What we can promise is the work we agree on and an honest report on what it did.

How quickly will I see results?

The audit is ready 48–72 hours after we have what we need from you.

Changes in the answers take longer, usually weeks to months, depending on your site, your competitors and the platforms. That is why we measure a starting point first and track from there.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. We start with the site you have and work out what can be fixed inside it before anyone mentions a rebuild.

What happens during the free snapshot?

We ask ChatGPT and Google three questions your customers would ask, look at the answers together, and tell you whether a full audit is worth it.

It is a short first look and separate from the paid audit.

Is there a minimum contract?

Growth and Authority run for at least three months, because technical and content changes need that long to show up.

After that you go month to month with 30 days’ notice. The audit is a one-off payment with nothing ongoing.

How will we track progress?

Every month you get a written report: what we did, the same customer questions asked again, and any change in mentions, recommendations and cited links.

If your analytics allow it, we add visits and enquiries that came from AI tools and search.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the booking form and pick “Custom quote”. Tell us about your sites, markets and goals. We reply within one business day, either with questions or with a written proposal.

Which markets do you cover?

We work remotely with businesses anywhere in the European Union. We test in English and Dutch, and compare answers between countries when that matters for you.

Plans that cover several countries are quoted as a Custom plan. See working across the EU.
