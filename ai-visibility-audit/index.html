Scope

what the audit is designed to answer.

The audit establishes a dated starting point. It separates brand mentions, recommendations, website links, and cited sources so different outcomes are not presented as the same thing.

01

does your brand appear?

We record whether the business is mentioned, recommended, linked, described inaccurately, or absent.

02

who appears instead?

Named competitors are tested against the same question set to show the comparative answer landscape.

03

which sources shape the answer?

Where sources are shown, we record the pages and domains referenced by the platform.

Process

documented from question to recommendation.

Every recommendation connects to an observed answer, a cited source, or a specific issue in your public business information or website.

01

define the questions

We agree on the services, locations, customer situations, and named competitors that make the test relevant.

02

run and record the checks

We test 15–20 customer questions across four platforms and record the date, answer context, source links, and result type.

03

compare and prioritise

We group the gaps, identify what can be changed, and order recommendations by likely usefulness and implementation effort.

04

explain the findings

You receive the evidence, a prioritised action list, and a direct review of what the audit does and does not show.

Discuss your audit ↗

AI answers can vary between searches, accounts, locations, and dates. The audit documents the checks performed; it does not guarantee rankings, mentions, recommendations, traffic, or sales.

Questions

before you commission an audit.

What information do you need?
Your website, core services, target customer situations, relevant locations, and the competitors you want included.
Is this the same as an SEO audit?
No. There is overlap in technical accessibility and useful content, but this audit also tests AI-generated answers and compares visibility across named businesses.
Will you guarantee a recommendation?
No. The platforms control their answers. Zenith commits to an agreed testing scope, documented evidence, and transparent recommendations.
What happens after the audit?
You can implement the priorities internally or discuss an ongoing Growth or Authority engagement with Zenith.
