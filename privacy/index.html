Privacy

how we handle your information.

This policy explains what information Zenith collects through this website, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Updated 3 October 2026

Information you provide

only what is needed for the conversation.

When you request a visibility snapshot, the form asks for your name, work email, business name, website, the problem you are facing, and an optional competitor. Zenith uses this information to review your request, prepare for the conversation, and send booking details.

01

booking details

Contact and business information submitted through the booking form.

02

communications

Messages you send and the information needed to answer them.

03

basic technical data

Hosting and security providers may process information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs to deliver and protect the website.

Use and sharing

used to provide the service—not sold.

Zenith uses submitted information to respond to requests, arrange calls, provide agreed services, maintain website security, and meet legal obligations. Personal information is not sold.

Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, email delivery, booking, and video calls. Calendly receives information you choose to provide when you follow a booking link; its own privacy terms apply. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect legal rights.

Analytics and cookies

analytics only with your consent.

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the site, such as which pages are viewed and which booking buttons are clicked. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) that are kept for up to two years.

Analytics cookies are only set if you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser so we do not ask again. Google processes this data as our service provider and may transfer it outside the UK and EEA under its own safeguards. Vercel Web Analytics also provides aggregated page-view statistics without cookies.

Retention and choices

keep control of your details.

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, legitimate business records, security, or legal requirements.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held by Zenith, subject to applicable law. Email takshdange@gmail.com to send your request.
