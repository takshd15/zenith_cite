Mark

a single mark, standing for the peak itself.

The wordmark is set entirely in lowercase Space Grotesk, led by a small solid square — a single, quiet accent standing in for the summit. The square never changes shape and never gains a second element.

Minimum clearspace: the height of the square mark on all sides. Minimum size: 18px tall in digital use, 0.35in in print. On the holographic background, the mark always renders in solid Ink Indigo — never in a lighter color, and never with a gradient fill of its own.
DON'T

Capitalize the wordmark. It is always lowercase "zenith," including at the start of a sentence in headlines.

DON'T

Fill the mark or wordmark with the holographic gradient — the gradient lives on backgrounds, not on the logo itself.

DON'T

Stretch, rotate, or round the square mark into a circle. It stays a fixed, upright square.

DON'T

Set the wordmark in anything but Space Grotesk — no substituting a system sans under deadline.

Voice

confident, evidence-first, never hypey.

The brand earns trust by showing proof, not by promising outcomes it can't guarantee. Every claim should be checkable.

show, don't sell

Lead with a specific, checkable fact rather than a superlative.

"You weren't mentioned once across 12 AI answers this month."

plain over technical

Explain schema, citations, and AI mechanics the way you'd explain it to a smart neighbor.

"Think of this as teaching AI what your business actually does."

steady, not salesy

No urgency tactics, no exclamation points doing the persuading.

"Here's what changed this month, and here's what we're doing next."
