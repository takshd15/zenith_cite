Findability

improve your AI findability.

AI findability is how easily an AI assistant can locate your business, understand what you offer and describe it accurately when a customer asks for a recommendation.

A business can rank well in Google and still be missing, outdated or misdescribed in AI answers. Zenith finds out why and fixes what is within your control.

Found, understood, described correctly.

Being findable means more than being indexed. AI tools also need to understand you and find other sources that agree with you.

Three questions

what it depends on.

We assess findability in three layers. A weakness in any one of them can keep a strong business out of the answer.

01

can AI tools reach you?

Search crawlers such as Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot need access to your pages, and important content should not depend on scripts that fail to load.

02

can they understand you?

Clear service pages that say who you help, where you work, what you charge and what makes you different are easier to summarise correctly.

03

can they trust you?

Independent sources such as reviews, directories, industry listings and press help confirm what your own website says.

Where to start

it starts with a findability check.

Effective optimization for AI search engines begins with evidence. We check the basics before recommending new content.

01

crawler access

Review robots.txt, server responses, redirects and rendering to confirm that search and AI crawlers can reach the pages that matter.

02

business consistency

Compare your name, description, services and contact details across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and key directories.

03

answer accuracy

Ask AI assistants about your business and record what they get wrong, such as outdated services, prices or locations.

04

prioritised fixes

Deliver a short list of changes, ordered by likely usefulness and the effort needed to make them.

Common causes

why AI tools miss a good business.

These are the issues we see most often when a business is absent from answers where it clearly belongs.

Access

blocked or hidden pages

Crawlers blocked by accident, key pages without internal links or content that only appears after a script runs.

Clarity

vague service descriptions

Pages that describe the company in general terms but never state the specific services, sectors or locations it covers.

Corroboration

few independent mentions

Little coverage outside your own website, or listings with conflicting details that make the business harder to verify.

Questions

common questions.

Is AI findability the same as AI visibility?
They are closely related. Findability focuses on whether AI tools can reach and understand your business. AI visibility measures whether you actually appear in the answers. Better findability is usually the first step towards better visibility.
Should I block AI crawlers?
That is a business decision. Blocking a search crawler such as OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot can stop your pages from appearing as sources in that platform’s answers. We explain the trade-offs for each crawler before you decide.
Do I need an llms.txt file?
It can be a useful summary, but it is not a requirement and does not replace clear, crawlable pages. We treat it as optional.
